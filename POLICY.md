At Buckwheat, one of my main priorities is the privacy of users.

## Information i collect

Most of what you type into Buckwheat never leaves your device. Your budgets, transactions, comments
and categories are stored in a local database on your phone, and the app works fully with no network
at all. The following are the exceptions, and each one happens only if you turn it on.

**Optional family sync.** If you create or join a family, your transactions, comments, categories and
your display name are uploaded to the family sync server *you* configure, so that the other people in
your family can see them. The app has no built-in server and ships with no host configured: a family
only exists once you point the app at a server you control. Every member of a family can see all of
another member's spending, including comments, and the app says so before you enrol. Leaving a
family revokes your access token and detaches your rows from that family on your device.

**Common household spending.** If your family turns this on, an expense recorded as household money —
rent, insurance, and so on — is visible to the head of the family in full. Everyone else sees only the
household total, not the individual entries, unless your family opts in to sharing them. The head can
also record a spend against another member's budget; that member is asked to accept it, and it only
counts once they do.

**Optional AI features.** If you configure an AI provider, the app sends spending data to *that*
provider to categorise transactions, generate a monthly summary, or interpret a voice note. The
provider is one you enter yourself; I do not operate it and do not receive what is sent. The key you
enter is stored on your device and is excluded from every backup. For family summaries specifically,
member display names are never included in the request: the app refers to people as "Member A",
"Member B" and so on, so a prompt cannot be used to work out who spent what.

**Spending tags.** The "super saver" style labels a family can see for each member are computed
entirely on your device from your own transactions. They are never uploaded, never stored on a
server, and never sent to an AI provider. Only the head of the family sees them in full, and a
member is only ever shown positive labels about themselves.

If you contact me directly, i may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, phone model,
the contents of the message and/or attachments you may send us, and any other information you may choose to provide.

## Log Files

Buckwheat follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log some errors when app crashes.
The information collected by log files include, phone type, operation system name, date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.
These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.
The purpose of the information is for analyzing errors in app, tracking users' movement in the app.

## Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

I don't use show any ads in my app. And my app don't collect any user tracking information

## What is not collected

No analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash-reporting service, and no account. There is
nothing to delete on my side, because there is nothing on my side. Backup files are written only to
where you tell the app to write them, and are encrypted with the passphrase you choose.
